# ISO 9000:2015 Quality Management Systems - Fundamentals and Vocabulary

Follow ISO 9000:2015 for all quality management systems work.

## Seven Quality Management Principles

1. **Customer Focus**
   - Primary focus: meet customer requirements, strive to exceed expectations
   - Key benefits: increased customer value/satisfaction/loyalty, enhanced reputation, expanded customer base
   - Actions: recognize direct/indirect customers, understand needs, link objectives to customer needs, communicate needs throughout organization

2. **Leadership**
   - Leaders establish unity of purpose, direction, create conditions for engagement
   - Key benefits: increased effectiveness/efficiency, better coordination, improved communication
   - Actions: communicate mission/vision/strategy, create shared values/trust, establish culture of integrity, inspire/recognize people

3. **Engagement of People**
   - Competent, empowered, engaged people at all levels essential
   - Key benefits: improved understanding/motivation, enhanced involvement/initiative/creativity, enhanced trust/collaboration
   - Actions: communicate importance of contribution, promote collaboration, facilitate open discussion, empower people to take initiatives

4. **Process Approach**
   - Consistent, predictable results via interrelated processes as coherent system
   - Key benefits: focused effort on key processes, coordinated processes, optimized performance
   - Actions: define system/process objectives, establish authority/accountability, understand interdependencies, manage as system

5. **Improvement**
   - Ongoing focus on improvement for sustained success
   - Key benefits: improved performance/satisfaction, enhanced root cause investigation, increased ability to anticipate/react
   - Actions: promote improvement objectives, educate/train people, develop/deploy improvement processes, track/review/audit projects

6. **Evidence-based Decision Making**
   - Decisions based on analysis/evaluation of data/information more likely to produce desired results
   - Key benefits: improved decision-making processes, improved assessment of performance, increased ability to review/challenge opinions
   - Actions: determine/measure/monitor key indicators, make data available, ensure accuracy/reliability, analyze using suitable methods

7. **Relationship Management**
   - Manage relationships with relevant interested parties (providers, partners) for sustained success
   - Key benefits: enhanced performance, common understanding of objectives/values, increased capability to create value
   - Actions: determine relevant interested parties, prioritize relationships, establish collaborative development, encourage improvements

## Fundamental Concepts

### Quality
- Degree to which inherent characteristics fulfill requirements
- Includes intended function/performance, perceived value/benefit to customer

### Quality Management System (QMS)
- Comprises activities to identify objectives, determine processes/resources
- Manages interacting processes/resources to provide value, realize results
- Enables top management to optimize resources considering consequences

### Context of Organization
- Combination of internal (values, culture, knowledge, performance) and external (legal, technological, competitive, market, cultural, social, economic) factors
- Determines organization's purpose, objectives, sustainability

### Interested Parties
- Extends beyond customer: any person/organization that can affect, be affected by, or perceive itself to be affected
- Relevant parties: those providing significant risk if needs/expectations not met

### Support Elements
- **People**: Essential resources; performance depends on behavior within system
- **Competence**: Skills, training, education, experience needed to perform roles
- **Awareness**: Understanding responsibilities, how actions contribute to objectives
- **Communication**: Planned internal/external communication enhances engagement

## QMS Development Model

### System Level
- Understand context, identify needs/expectations of interested parties
- Develop QMS to achieve organizational sustainability
- Outputs from one process = inputs to others (interlinked network)
- Unique to each organization

### Process Level
- Defined, measured, improved activities delivering results consistent with objectives
- Cross functional boundaries; some critical, others not
- Activities with inputs delivering outputs

### Activity Level
- People collaborate within processes to carry out daily activities
- Some prescribed (understand objectives), others react to external stimuli

## Key Terms by Category

### Person/People (3.1)
- **Top management**: Person/group directing/controlling organization at highest level
- **Quality management system consultant**: Assists on QMS realization
- **Involvement**: Taking part in activity/event/situation
- **Engagement**: Involvement/contribution to activities to achieve shared objectives
- **Configuration authority**: Person(s) with responsibility/authority for configuration decisions
- **Dispute resolver**: Individual assigned to assist parties in resolving disputes

### Organization (3.2)
- **Organization**: Person/group with functions, responsibilities, authorities, relationships to achieve objectives
- **Context of the organization**: Internal/external issues affecting approach to objectives
- **Interested party** (stakeholder): Person/organization affecting/affected by decision/activity
- **Customer**: Person/organization receiving product/service intended/required
- **Provider** (supplier): Organization providing product/service
- **External provider**: Provider not part of organization
- **DRP-provider**: Dispute resolution process provider
- **Association**: Organization consisting of member organizations/persons
- **Metrological function**: Unit responsible for measurement management system

### Activity (3.3)
- **Improvement**: Activity to enhance performance (continual: recurring)
- **Management**: Coordinated activities to direct/control organization
- **Quality management**: Management with regard to quality
- **Quality planning**: Setting quality objectives, specifying operational processes/resources
- **Quality assurance**: Focused on providing confidence that quality requirements will be fulfilled
- **Quality control**: Focused on fulfilling quality requirements
- **Quality improvement**: Focused on increasing ability to fulfill quality requirements
- **Configuration management**: Coordinated activities to direct/control configuration
- **Change control**: Activities for control of output after approval
- **Project management**: Planning, organizing, monitoring, controlling, reporting project activities
- **Configuration object**: Object within configuration satisfying end-use function
- **Competence acquisition**: Process of attaining competence

### Process (3.4)
- **Process**: Set of interrelated/interacting activities using inputs to deliver intended result
- **Project**: Unique process with start/finish dates to achieve objective
- **QMS realization**: Process of establishing, documenting, implementing, maintaining, improving QMS
- **Procedure**: Specified way to carry out activity/process
- **Outsource**: Arrangement where external organization performs part of function/process
- **Contract**: Binding agreement
- **Design and development**: Set of processes transforming requirements into detailed requirements

### System (3.5)
- **System**: Set of interrelated/interacting elements
- **Infrastructure**: System of facilities, equipment, services needed for operation
- **Management system**: Set of elements to establish policies/objectives, processes to achieve them
- **QMS**: Part of management system with regard to quality
- **Work environment**: Set of conditions under which work is performed
- **Metrological confirmation**: Operations to ensure measuring equipment conforms to requirements
- **Measurement management system**: Elements necessary to achieve metrological confirmation
- **Policy**: Intentions/direction as formally expressed by top management
- **Quality policy**: Policy related to quality
- **Vision**: Aspiration of what organization would like to become
- **Mission**: Purpose for existing
- **Strategy**: Plan to achieve long-term/overall objective

### Requirement (3.6)
- **Object** (entity/item): Anything perceivable/conceivable (material, non-material, imagined)
- **Quality**: Degree to which inherent characteristics fulfill requirements
- **Grade**: Category/rank given to requirements with same functional use
- **Requirement**: Need/expectation that is stated, implied, or obligatory
- **Quality requirement**: Requirement related to quality
- **Statutory requirement**: Obligatory requirement specified by legislative body
- **Regulatory requirement**: Obligatory requirement specified by mandated authority
- **Product configuration information**: Requirement/other information for product design/operation/support
- **Nonconformity**: Non-fulfillment of requirement
- **Defect**: Nonconformity related to intended/specified use
- **Conformity**: Fulfilment of requirement
- **Capability**: Ability of object to realize output fulfilling requirements
- **Traceability**: Ability to trace history, application, location of object
- **Dependability**: Ability to perform as/when required
- **Innovation**: New/changed object realizing/redistributing value

### Result (3.7)
- **Objective**: Result to be achieved (strategic, tactical, operational)
- **Quality objective**: Objective related to quality
- **Success**: Achievement of objective
- **Sustained success**: Success over period of time (balance economic-financial/social/ecological)
- **Output**: Result of a process (product: tangible, service: intangible)
- **Product**: Output that can be produced without transaction (dominant: tangible)
- **Service**: Output with at least one activity between organization and customer (dominant: intangible)
- **Performance**: Measurable result (effectiveness: extent objectives achieved, efficiency: relationship result vs. resources used)
- **Risk**: Effect of uncertainty (positive/negative deviation from expected)
- **Effectiveness**: Extent to which planned activities realized, planned results achieved
- **Efficiency**: Relationship between result achieved and resources used

### Data/Information/Document (3.8)
- **Data**: Facts about an object
- **Information**: Meaningful data
- **Objective evidence**: Data supporting existence/verity of something
- **Information system**: Network of communication channels within organization
- **Document**: Information and medium on which it is contained (record, specification, drawing, report)
- **Documented information**: Information required to be controlled/maintained by organization
- **Specification**: Document stating requirements (quality manual, quality plan, procedure)
- **Quality manual**: Specification for QMS of organization
- **Quality plan**: Specification of procedures/resources for specific object
- **Record**: Document stating results achieved/evidence of activities performed
- **Project management plan**: Document specifying what necessary to meet project objectives
- **Verification**: Confirmation through objective evidence that requirements fulfilled
- **Validation**: Confirmation through objective evidence that requirements for intended use fulfilled
- **Configuration status accounting**: Recording/reporting product configuration information, change status
- **Specific case**: Subject of quality plan

### Customer (3.9)
- **Feedback**: Opinions/comments/expressions of interest in product/service/complaints process
- **Customer satisfaction**: Customer's perception of degree to which expectations fulfilled
- **Complaint**: Expression of dissatisfaction with expectation of response/resolution
- **Customer service**: Interaction with customer throughout life cycle
- **Customer satisfaction code of conduct**: Promises to customers concerning behavior
- **Dispute**: Disagreement from complaint submitted to DRP-provider

### Characteristic (3.10)
- **Characteristic**: Distinguishing feature (physical, sensory, behavioral, temporal, ergonomic, functional)
- **Quality characteristic**: Inherent characteristic related to requirement
- **Human factor**: Characteristic of person impacting object (physical, cognitive, social)
- **Competence**: Ability to apply knowledge/skills to achieve intended results
- **Metrological characteristic**: Characteristic influencing measurement results
- **Configuration**: Interrelated functional/physical characteristics of product/service
- **Configuration baseline**: Approved configuration information establishing characteristics at point in time

### Determination (3.11)
- **Determination**: Activity to find out characteristics/values
- **Review**: Determination of suitability/adequacy/effectiveness to achieve objectives
- **Monitoring**: Determining status of system/process/product/service
- **Measurement**: Process to determine value
- **Measurement process**: Set of operations to determine quantity value
- **Measuring equipment**: Instrument, software, standard, reference material for measurement
- **Inspection**: Determination of conformity to specified requirements
- **Test**: Determination according to requirements for intended use/application
- **Progress evaluation**: Assessment of progress toward project objectives

### Action (3.12)
- **Preventive action**: Action to eliminate cause of potential nonconformity (prevent occurrence)
- **Corrective action**: Action to eliminate cause of nonconformity (prevent recurrence)
- **Correction**: Action to eliminate detected nonconformity
- **Regrade**: Alteration of grade to make nonconforming product/service conform
- **Concession** (deviation permit): Permission to use/release nonconforming product/service
- **Release**: Permission to proceed to next stage/process
- **Rework**: Action on nonconforming product/service to make it conform
- **Repair**: Action on nonconforming product/service to make it acceptable for intended use
- **Scrap**: Action to preclude originally intended use

### Audit (3.13)
- **Audit**: Systematic, independent, documented process for obtaining/evaluating objective evidence
  - **Internal audit** (first party): By/for organization itself
  - **External audit** (second/third party): By parties with interest, or independent auditing organizations
  - **Combined audit**: Two+ management systems at single auditee
  - **Joint audit**: Two+ auditing organizations at single auditee
- **Audit programme**: Set of audits planned for specific time frame/purpose
- **Audit scope**: Extent/boundaries of audit
- **Audit plan**: Description of activities/arrangements for audit
- **Audit criteria**: Set of policies/procedures/requirements as reference
- **Audit evidence**: Records/statements/information relevant to criteria, verifiable
- **Audit findings**: Results of evaluation of evidence against criteria (conformity/nonconformity)
- **Audit conclusion**: Outcome after consideration of objectives/findings
- **Audit client**: Organization/person requesting audit
- **Auditee**: Organization being audited
- **Guide**: Person appointed to assist audit team
- **Audit team**: One+ persons conducting audit (led by auditor, may include experts)
- **Auditor**: Person who conducts audit
- **Technical expert**: Person providing specific knowledge/expertise to team
- **Observer**: Person accompanying team but not acting as auditor

## Concept Relationships (Annex A)

- **Generic relations**: Fan/tree diagram (no arrows) - suboardinate inherits superordinate characteristics
  - Example: season → spring, summer, autumn, winter
  
- **Partitive relations**: Rake diagram (no arrows) - parts constitute whole, no inheritance
  - Example: year → spring, summer, autumn, winter
  
- **Associative relations**: Line with arrowheads at each end - thematic connections
  - Example: sunshine → summer, swimming

## Integrated Management Systems

QMS can be integrated with:
- Environmental management (ISO 14001)
- Energy management (ISO 50001)
- Occupational health and safety
- Information security (ISO/IEC 27001)
- Financial, growth, profitability aspects

Single management system approach more effective/efficient than separate systems.
