# ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management Systems - Requirements

Follow ISO 9001:2015 for implementing, maintaining, and improving a Quality Management System (QMS).

## Scope

Specifies requirements for a QMS when an organization needs to:
- Consistently provide products/services meeting customer & applicable statutory/regulatory requirements
- Enhance customer satisfaction through effective QMS application

**Applies to**: Any organization regardless of type, size, or products/services provided.

## Core Concepts

### Process Approach with PDCA Cycle
- **Plan**: Establish objectives, processes, resources; identify risks & opportunities
- **Do**: Implement what was planned
- **Check**: Monitor, measure, analyze, evaluate performance
- **Act**: Take actions for improvement

### Risk-based Thinking
- Implicit in all clauses
- Address both risks (negative effects) and opportunities (positive effects)
- Plan actions, integrate into processes, evaluate effectiveness

## Clause 4: Context of the Organization

### 4.1 Understanding the Organization and its Context
- Determine external issues (legal, technological, competitive, market, cultural, social, economic)
- Determine internal issues (values, culture, knowledge, performance)
- Monitor & review information about these issues

### 4.2 Understanding Needs & Expectations of Interested Parties
- Determine relevant interested parties (customers, providers, owners, employees, regulators, partners)
- Determine their relevant requirements
- Monitor & review information about them

### 4.3 Determining Scope of QMS
- Consider external/internal issues, interested party requirements, products/services
- Apply all requirements within determined scope
- Maintain scope as documented information
- Justify any requirements determined not applicable

### 4.4 Quality Management System & its Processes
- Establish, implement, maintain, continually improve QMS
- Determine processes needed & their interactions
- Determine & apply criteria/methods for monitoring/measurement
- Determine resources needed & ensure availability
- Assign responsibilities & authorities
- Address risks & opportunities per Clause 6.1
- Evaluate processes & implement changes needed
- Maintain documented information for operation
- Retain documented information for confidence processes are carried out

## Clause 5: Leadership

### 5.1 Leadership & Commitment
Top management shall demonstrate leadership & commitment by:
- Taking accountability for QMS effectiveness
- Ensuring quality policy & objectives established & compatible with strategic direction
- Ensuring QMS requirements integrated into business processes
- Promoting process approach & risk-based thinking
- Ensuring resources needed are available
- Communicating importance of effective QMS & conformity
- Ensuring QMS achieves intended results
- Engaging, directing & supporting persons
- Promoting improvement
- Supporting other relevant management roles

### 5.2 Policy
Top management shall establish, implement & maintain quality policy that:
- Is appropriate to purpose & context, supports strategic direction
- Provides framework for setting quality objectives
- Includes commitment to satisfy applicable requirements
- Includes commitment to continual QMS improvement

**Communicate**: Available as documented information, communicated & understood within organization, available to relevant interested parties

### 5.3 Organizational Roles, Responsibilities & Authorities
Top management shall:
- Assign responsibilities & authorities for relevant roles
- Ensure responsibility for: conformity to requirements, processes delivering outputs, reporting QMS performance & improvement opportunities, promoting customer focus, maintaining QMS integrity during changes

## Clause 6: Planning

### 6.1 Actions to Address Risks & Opportunities
Consider issues (4.1) & requirements (4.2) to determine risks & opportunities:
- Give assurance QMS achieves intended results
- Enhance desirable effects
- Prevent/reduce undesired effects
- Achieve improvement

**Plan actions**: Integrate into QMS processes, evaluate effectiveness. Actions proportionate to potential impact.

**Options**: Avoid risk, take risk for opportunity, eliminate source, change likelihood/consequences, share risk, retain risk by informed decision.

### 6.2 Quality Objectives & Planning to Achieve Them
Establish quality objectives at relevant functions, levels & processes:
- Consistent with quality policy
- Measurable
- Take into account applicable requirements
- Relevant to conformity & customer satisfaction enhancement
- Monitored
- Communicated
- Updated as appropriate

**Planning**: Determine what will be done, resources needed, responsible persons, timeline, evaluation method. Retain documented information on objectives.

### 6.3 Planning of Changes
When changing QMS, carry out in planned manner. Consider:
- Purpose of changes & potential consequences
- Integrity of QMS
- Availability of resources
- Allocation/reallocation of responsibilities & authorities

## Clause 7: Support

### 7.1 Resources
Determine & provide resources for QMS establishment, implementation, maintenance & improvement:
- Capabilities & constraints of internal resources
- What needs to be obtained from external providers

**Specific resources**:
- **People**: Determine & provide persons for effective QMS implementation & process operation
- **Infrastructure**: Buildings, utilities, equipment (hardware/software), transportation, ICT
- **Environment**: Social (non-discriminatory, calm), psychological (stress-reducing), physical (temperature, humidity, light, airflow, hygiene, noise)
- **Monitoring & measuring resources**: Ensure valid/reliable results; calibrate/verify against standards; identify status; safeguard from adjustments/damage
- **Organizational knowledge**: Determine, provide, maintain knowledge (internal sources: IP, experience, lessons, results; external: standards, academia, conferences, customers, providers)

### 7.2 Competence
- Determine necessary competence for persons doing work under control
- Ensure persons competent based on education, training, experience
- Take actions to acquire competence & evaluate effectiveness
- Retain documented information as evidence of competence

### 7.3 Awareness
Ensure persons doing work under control are aware of:
- Quality policy
- Relevant quality objectives
- Their contribution to QMS effectiveness
- Implications of not conforming with QMS requirements

### 7.4 Communication
Determine internal & external communications re: what, when, with whom, how, who communicates.

### 7.5 Documented Information
**QMS shall include**: Documented information required by standard + determined by organization as necessary.

**Creating & updating**: Ensure identification/description, format, review/approval for suitability.

**Control**: For required documented information, ensure:
- Available & suitable for use where/when needed
- Adequately protected (confidentiality, improper use, integrity loss)
- Control activities: distribution, access, retrieval, use, storage, preservation, control of changes (version control), retention, disposition
- External origin documented information identified as necessary shall be controlled
- Documented information retained as evidence shall be protected from unintended alterations

## Clause 8: Operation

### 8.1 Operational Planning & Control
Plan, implement & control processes needed to meet requirements for products/services:
- Determine requirements for products/services
- Establish criteria for processes & acceptance
- Determine resources needed
- Implement control per criteria
- Determine, maintain, retain documented information to: have confidence processes carried out; demonstrate conformity
- Control planned changes, review consequences, mitigate adverse effects
- Ensure outsourced processes controlled

### 8.2 Requirements for Products & Services

**8.2.1 Customer Communication**: Provide information, handle enquiries/orders/changes, obtain feedback (including complaints), control customer property, establish contingency actions.

**8.2.2 Determine Requirements**: Define requirements including applicable statutory/regulatory & those considered necessary. Determine organization can meet claims.

**8.2.3 Review Requirements**: Review before committing to supply. Review: customer requirements (including delivery/post-delivery), organization requirements, statutory/regulatory requirements. Resolve differing requirements. Retain documented information on review & new requirements.

**8.2.4 Changes**: Ensure relevant documented information amended, relevant persons aware of changed requirements.

### 8.3 Design & Development

**8.3.1 General**: Establish, implement & maintain design/development process appropriate for subsequent provision.

**8.3.2 Planning**: Determine stages/controls considering: nature/duration/complexity, required reviews/verification/validation, responsibilities/authorities, resource needs, interface control, customer/user involvement, subsequent provision needs, level of control expected, documented information needed.

**8.3.3 Inputs**: Determine essential requirements. Consider: functional/performance requirements, info from previous similar activities, statutory/regulatory, standards/codes committed to, risk of failure. Inputs adequate, complete, unambiguous. Resolve conflicting inputs. Retain documented information.

**8.3.4 Controls**: Apply controls to ensure: results defined, reviews conducted, verification activities, validation activities, actions on problems determined. Retain documented information.

**8.3.5 Outputs**: Ensure outputs: meet input requirements, adequate for subsequent processes, include monitoring/measurement requirements & acceptance criteria, specify characteristics essential for intended purpose & safe provision. Retain documented information.

**8.3.6 Changes**: Identify, review & control changes. Retain documented information on changes, review results, authorization, actions taken.

### 8.4 Control of Externally Provided Processes, Products & Services

**8.4.1 General**: Ensure externally provided processes/products/services conform to requirements.

**8.4.2 Type & Extent of Control**: Ensure externally provided processes/products/services don't adversely affect ability to consistently deliver conforming products/services. Determine & apply criteria for evaluation/selection/monitoring/re-evaluation based on ability to provide. Consider: potential impact, effectiveness of controls applied by provider, verification/other activities needed. Retain documented information.

**8.4.3 Information for External Providers**: Determine requirements for: processes/products/services, approval of methods/processes/equipment, personnel competence, provider interactions, performance monitoring/measurement, verification/validation at provider premises.

### 8.5 Production & Service Provision

**8.5.1 Control**: Implement under controlled conditions: documented information defining characteristics/results, monitoring/measurement resources, infrastructure/environment, competent persons, validation of processes where output can't be verified, actions to prevent human error, release/delivery/post-delivery activities.

**8.5.2 Identification & Traceability**: Use suitable means to identify outputs. Identify status wrt monitoring/measurement requirements. Control unique identification when traceability required. Retain documented information for traceability.

**8.5.3 Property Belonging to Customers/External Providers**: Exercise care with customer/provider property. Identify, verify, protect, safeguard. Report lost/damaged/unsuitable property. Property includes: materials, components, tools, equipment, premises, IP, personal data.

**8.5.4 Preservation**: Preserve outputs during production/service provision: identification, handling, contamination control, packaging, storage, transmission/transportation, protection.

**8.5.5 Post-delivery Activities**: Meet requirements for post-delivery activities. Consider: statutory/regulatory, potential undesired consequences, nature/use/lifetime, customer requirements, customer feedback.

**8.5.6 Control of Changes**: Review & control changes to production/service provision. Retain documented information on review results, person authorizing change, actions arising.

**8.6 Release of Products & Services**: Implement planned arrangements to verify requirements met. Don't release until planned arrangements satisfactorily completed (unless approved by relevant authority & customer). Retain documented information on release including conformity evidence & traceability to person authorizing release.

### 8.7 Control of Nonconforming Outputs
- Identify & control nonconforming outputs to prevent unintended use/delivery
- Take appropriate action based on nature & effect (correction, segregation/containment/return/suspension, inform customer, obtain concession)
- Verify conformity when corrected
- Deal with nonconforming outputs: correction (rework, regrade), concession (permission to use/release), scrap (preclude intended use)
- Retain documented information: describes nonconformity, actions taken, concessions obtained, authority deciding action

## Clause 9: Performance Evaluation

### 9.1 Monitoring, Measurement, Analysis & Evaluation

**9.1.1 General**: Determine: what needs to be monitored/measured, methods for valid results, when to perform, when to analyze/evaluate results. Evaluate QMS performance & effectiveness. Retain documented information as evidence of results.

**9.1.2 Customer Satisfaction**: Monitor customer perceptions of degree to which expectations fulfilled. Determine methods for obtaining/monitoring/evaluating. Use: customer surveys, feedback, meetings, market share analysis, compliments, warranty claims, dealer reports.

**9.1.3 Analysis & Evaluation**: Analyze & evaluate data/information. Evaluate: conformity of products/services, degree of customer satisfaction, QMS performance/effectiveness, planning effectiveness, risks/opportunities addressed, performance of external providers, need for improvements.

### 9.2 Internal Audit

**9.2.1 General**: Conduct internal audits at planned intervals to provide info on whether QMS:
- Conforms to own requirements, requirements of ISO 9001
- Is effectively implemented & maintained

**9.2.2 Internal Audit Programme**: Plan, establish, implement, maintain audit programme(s) considering: importance of processes, changes affecting organization, results of previous audits. Define: frequency, methods, responsibilities, planning requirements, reporting. Ensure auditors/audit team objective & impartial (not responsible for audited activity). Report results to management. Take correction & corrective actions without undue delay. Retain documented information on audit programme & results.

Consider combining with other management system audits (combined audit) or joint audits with other auditing organizations.

### 9.3 Management Review

**9.3.1 General**: Review QMS at planned intervals for continuing suitability, adequacy, effectiveness, alignment with strategic direction.

**9.3.2 Inputs**: Consider: results of previous management reviews, changes in external/internal issues, information on QMS performance (trends in customer satisfaction, conformity, nonconformities/corrective actions, monitoring/measurement results, audit results, external provider performance, resource adequacy), adequacy of resources, opportunities for improvement.

**9.3.3 Outputs**: Include decisions & actions related to: improvement opportunities, any needs for QMS changes, resource needs. Retain documented information as evidence of results.

## Clause 10: Improvement

### 10.1 General
Determine & select opportunities for improvement. Implement necessary actions to: meet requirements & enhance customer satisfaction, correct/prevent/reduce undesired effects, improve QMS performance/effectiveness. Include: correction, corrective action, continual improvement, breakthrough change, innovation, re-organization.

### 10.2 Nonconformity & Corrective Action

**10.2.1**: When nonconformity occurs (including complaints):
- React & control it (take action, deal with consequences)
- Evaluate need for action to eliminate cause(s) (review/analyze, determine cause(s), determine if similar/likely nonconformities exist)
- Implement any action needed
- Review effectiveness of corrective action taken
- Update risks/opportunities per Clause 6.1 if necessary
- Make changes to QMS if necessary

Corrective actions proportionate to effects of nonconformities.

**10.2.2**: Retain documented information as evidence: nature of nonconformities & subsequent actions, results of corrective actions.

### 10.3 Continual Improvement
Continually improve suitability, adequacy, effectiveness of QMS. Consider results of analysis/evaluation, management review outputs to determine needs/opportunities for continual improvement.

## Annex A: Clarification of New Structure, Terminology & Concepts

### Key Changes from ISO 9001:2008
- New clause structure (4-10) aligned with other ISO management system standards
- Adoption of process approach with PDCA cycle & risk-based thinking
- Terminology changes: "documented information" replaces "documentation/records", "external provider" replaces "supplier", "interested parties" replaces "stakeholders", "goods/services" expanded to "products & services"

### No Requirement For...
- Uniformity in QMS structure
- Alignment of documentation to clause structure
- Use of specific terminology within organization

## Integration with Other Management Systems

Single integrated management system more effective/efficient than separate systems. Can integrate quality (ISO 9001), environmental (ISO 14001), energy (ISO 50001), OHS (ISO 45001), information security (ISO/IEC 27001) into one system.

**Benefits**: Common policies, streamlined processes, reduced duplication, optimized resource use, holistic view of risks/opportunities.

## Verification Checklist for ISO 9001:2015 Implementation

### Leadership (Clause 5)
□ Top management demonstrates leadership & commitment
□ Quality policy established, communicated, compatible with strategic direction
□ Responsibilities & authorities assigned, communicated
□ Customer focus maintained throughout organization

### Planning (Clause 6)
□ Risks & opportunities identified, actions planned & implemented
□ Quality objectives established (measurable, monitored, communicated)
□ Changes to QMS planned with consideration of consequences

### Support (Clause 7)
□ Resources (people, infrastructure, environment) determined & provided
□ Competence of persons ensured, evidence retained
□ Persons aware of policy, objectives, contribution, implications
□ Communication determined (what, when, with whom, how)
□ Documented information controlled (create, update, control)

### Operation (Clause 8)
□ Operational planning & control implemented
□ Customer requirements determined, reviewed, communicated
□ Design/development process established & controlled
□ External providers evaluated, selected, monitored
□ Production/service provision controlled
□ Nonconforming outputs identified & controlled

### Performance Evaluation (Clause 9)
□ Monitoring/measurement/analysis/evaluation conducted
□ Customer satisfaction monitored
□ Internal audits conducted per programme
□ Management review conducted at planned intervals

### Improvement (Clause 10)
□ Opportunities for improvement determined & acted on
□ Nonconformities & corrective actions addressed
□ Continual improvement implemented
