# Apply for Passport

Request a cattle passport for a registered bovine animal.

## When to Do This

A passport is issued automatically after birth registration and tagging for cattle. Use this page to request a replacement or if the automatic issuance did not complete.

## Requirements

- The animal must be registered and tagged in the system
- The animal must be a bovine (cattle)
- The birth registration must be complete

## Step-by-Step

1. Open the app → **Passports** tab
2. Tap **+** (new request)
3. Select **Apply for Passport**
4. Search for the animal by ear tag code
5. Verify the animal details (date of birth, breed, sex)
6. Confirm the request

> **Tip:** In most cases, the passport is issued automatically after tagging. Only use this page if you did not receive the passport or it was not issued.

## What Happens Next

- The passport request is submitted to the authorities
- The passport is issued and sent to your farm
- The passport status becomes **Active**
- You can view the digital version in the app
