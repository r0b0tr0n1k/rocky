# Account & Settings

Manage your Rocky account profile, notifications, and offline settings.

## What You Can Do

- **View profile** — See your account details and farm information
- **Manage notifications** — Configure which alerts you receive
- **Use offline mode** — Work without internet connection

## Key Concepts

- Your account is linked to your farm or veterinary practice
- Notifications keep you informed of important events
- Offline mode allows you to continue working without connectivity
