# Using the App Offline

Rocky supports offline operation, allowing you to continue working when you do not have an internet connection.

## When to Do This

You can use the app offline whenever you are in an area without internet access. This is common on farms in remote locations.

## How Offline Mode Works

- All essential data is stored locally on your device
- You can register births, record movements, and enter health data offline
- Data is queued and synced automatically when the connection is restored
- The app shows an offline indicator when you are not connected

## Step-by-Step

### Work Offline

1. Open the app normally
2. The offline indicator appears at the top of the screen
3. Continue using all features as normal
4. Data is stored locally on your device

### Sync When Connected

1. When internet connection is restored, the app syncs automatically
2. A progress indicator shows sync status
3. When sync is complete, the offline indicator disappears

> **Tip:** The app syncs in the background. You do not need to stay on the screen — just keep the app installed and data will sync.

## What Happens Next

- Your offline data is uploaded to the national registry
- Any conflicts are resolved (latest change wins)
- You will receive a confirmation when sync is complete
