# Market / Sale Movement

Record a movement when an animal is sold at a market or directly to another farm.

## When to Do This

Record a market or sale movement when:

- You sell an animal at a livestock market
- You sell an animal directly to another farm
- An animal changes ownership

## Requirements

- The animal must be registered and tagged
- The buyer's farm or the market must be registered in the system
- You have 7 days from the movement date to record it

## Step-by-Step

1. Open the app → **Movements** tab
2. Tap **+** (new movement)
3. Select **Market / Sale** as the movement type
4. Search for the animal by ear tag code
5. Enter the sale date
6. Select the destination: market or buyer's farm
7. Enter the sale price (optional)
8. Confirm the record

> **Tip:** If selling at a market, the market operator may record the movement. Confirm with the operator to avoid duplicate records.

## What Happens Next

- The animal is moved out of your farm inventory
- The buyer or market records the arrival
- The animal's passport (if cattle) follows the animal to the new owner
- The movement is recorded in the national registry
