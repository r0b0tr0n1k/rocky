# Record Death

Record an animal death that occurs on the farm, during transport, or at the slaughterhouse.

## When to Do This

Record a death whenever an animal dies, regardless of the cause or location. You have 7 days from the date of death to record it.

## Requirements

- The animal must be registered in the system
- You need to know the date and cause of death
- For slaughterhouse deaths: the slaughterhouse may record this on your behalf

## Step-by-Step

1. Open the app → **Movements** tab
2. Tap **+** (new movement)
3. Select **Death** as the movement type
4. Search for the animal by ear tag code
5. Enter the date of death
6. Select the cause of death:
   - Natural causes
   - Disease (if notifiable, see [Notifiable Diseases](/user-guide/health/notifiable-diseases))
   - Accident
   - Slaughtered (recorded by slaughterhouse)
   - Unknown
7. Add any relevant notes
8. Confirm the record

> **Tip:** If the death was due to a notifiable disease, report it immediately through the **Health** section — do not wait for the 7-day deadline.

## What Happens Next

- The animal's status is updated to **Deceased**
- The movement is recorded in the national registry
- If the animal had a passport, it is automatically cancelled
- The animal is removed from active farm counts
