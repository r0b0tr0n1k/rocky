# Pasture Movement

Record a change of pasture or grazing location for your animals.

## When to Do This

Record a pasture movement whenever your animals move to a different pasture or grazing area. This includes seasonal pasture changes and temporary grazing moves.

## Requirements

- The animal must be registered in the system
- You need to know the departure and destination locations
- You have 7 days from the movement date to record it

## Step-by-Step

1. Open the app → **Movements** tab
2. Tap **+** (new movement)
3. Select **Pasture** as the movement type
4. Search for the animal by ear tag code
5. Enter the date of the pasture change
6. Select the departure farm or location
7. Select the destination pasture location
8. Enter the expected duration (if temporary)
9. Add any relevant notes
10. Confirm the record

> **Tip:** For temporary pasture moves (e.g., seasonal grazing), you can record the expected return date. The system will remind you when the animal is due back.

## What Happens Next

- The animal's location is updated to the new pasture
- The movement is recorded in the national registry
- The animal remains in your farm inventory but at a different location
- When the animal returns, record the return movement the same way
