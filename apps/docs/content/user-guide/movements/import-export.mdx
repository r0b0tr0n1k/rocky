# Import & Export

Record cross-border movements when animals enter or leave North Macedonia.

## When to Do This

Record an import or export movement whenever an animal crosses the national border. This applies to both incoming (import) and outgoing (export) animals.

## Requirements

- The animal must be registered and tagged (for exports, the animal must already be in the system)
- You need the country of origin or destination
- Supporting documentation may be required (health certificates, import permits)
- You have 7 days from the border crossing date to record it

## Step-by-Step

1. Open the app → **Movements** tab
2. Tap **+** (new movement)
3. Select **Import** or **Export** as the movement type
4. Search for the animal by ear tag code
5. Enter the border crossing date
6. Select the country of origin (for imports) or destination (for exports)
7. Attach supporting documents if available
8. Enter any additional reference numbers (permit, certificate)
9. Confirm the record

> **Tip:** Cross-border movements often require advance approval from veterinary authorities. Plan ahead to avoid delays.

## What Happens Next

- The movement is recorded in the national registry
- For imports: the animal is added to your farm inventory
- For exports: the animal is removed from your farm inventory
- If the animal had a passport (cattle), it is updated or cancelled as appropriate
