# Send to Slaughter

Record a movement when an animal is sent to the slaughterhouse.

## When to Do This

Record a slaughter movement when you send an animal to a slaughterhouse for meat production. The slaughterhouse may also record the arrival on your behalf.

## Requirements

- The animal must be registered and tagged
- The slaughterhouse must be registered in the system
- You have 7 days from the movement date to record it

## Step-by-Step

1. Open the app → **Movements** tab
2. Tap **+** (new movement)
3. Select **Slaughter** as the movement type
4. Search for the animal by ear tag code
5. Enter the departure date
6. Select the destination slaughterhouse
7. Enter the reason (e.g., meat production, emergency slaughter)
8. Confirm the record

> **Tip:** The slaughterhouse may record the arrival themselves. Check the animal's movement history to confirm the record has been made.

## What Happens Next

- The animal is moved out of your farm inventory
- The slaughterhouse records the arrival and slaughter details
- The animal's passport (if cattle) is cancelled upon slaughter
- The movement is recorded in the national registry
