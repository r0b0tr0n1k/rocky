# Animals & Ear Tags

This section covers everything related to animal identification and registration in Rocky. You will learn how to register births, order ear tags, and manage your animal records.

## What You Can Do

- **Register a birth** — Record a new calf, lamb, or kid in the system
- **Order ear tags** — Request tags for new animals or replacements
- **Manage animals** — Search, view details, and correct animal records

## Key Rules

- Bovine animals must be tagged within 20 days of birth
- Ovine and caprine animals must be tagged within 30 days of birth
- Ear tags must be applied before any movement occurs
- All animals must have a passport (cattle only)
