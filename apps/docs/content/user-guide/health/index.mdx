# Health

This section covers health management in Rocky, including vaccinations, treatments, lab tests, and notifiable disease reporting.

## What You Can Do

- **Record vaccinations** — Log vaccines administered to your animals
- **Record treatments** — Document diagnoses and treatments
- **Request lab tests** — Submit samples and view results
- **Report notifiable diseases** — Immediately flag suspected outbreaks

## Key Rules

- Vaccinations must be recorded with batch number and expiry date
- Notifiable diseases must be reported immediately (do not wait for the 7-day movement deadline)
- Treatment records support disease surveillance and traceability
