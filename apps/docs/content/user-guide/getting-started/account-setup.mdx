# Account Setup

This page explains how to register, sign in, and sign out of the Rocky mobile app.

## When to Do This

You need a Rocky account before you can register animals, record movements, or use any other feature. Account setup happens once — after that, you simply sign in.

## Requirements

- A valid email address
- Your farm or veterinary practice registration number
- A smartphone with the Rocky app installed

## Step-by-Step

### Register a New Account

1. Open the Rocky app on your smartphone
2. Tap **Create Account**
3. Enter your email address
4. Enter your farm or practice registration number
5. Create a secure password (at least 8 characters)
6. Verify your email address using the link sent to your inbox
7. Complete your profile: name, role (farmer or veterinarian), and contact details

### Sign In

1. Open the Rocky app
2. Enter your email and password
3. Tap **Sign In**

### Sign Out

1. Tap your profile icon in the top-right corner
2. Scroll to the bottom
3. Tap **Sign Out**

## What Happens Next

- Your account is linked to your farm or veterinary practice
- You can now access all features according to your role
- Your session stays active until you sign out
