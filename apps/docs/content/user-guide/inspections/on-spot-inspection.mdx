# On-Spot Inspection

What happens during an on-spot inspection at your farm.

## When to Do This

An on-spot inspection happens when your farm is selected by the annual risk analysis. You will be notified in advance of the scheduled visit.

## Requirements

- You must be present during the inspection (or have a representative)
- You need access to your animal records
- The inspector (veterinarian) will verify animal identification and records

## What the Inspector Checks

The veterinarian will verify:

1. **Animal identification** — Ear tags match the registered codes
2. **Animal count** — Number of animals matches the registry
3. **Movement records** — All movements are recorded correctly and on time
4. **Health records** — Vaccinations, treatments, and disease reports are up to date
5. **Farm book** — The physical or digital farm book is maintained
6. **General compliance** — Any other regulatory requirements

## Step-by-Step

### Prepare for the Inspection

1. Review your animal records and ensure they are accurate
2. Verify all recent movements have been recorded
3. Ensure all ear tags are in place and readable
4. Have your farm book and supporting documents ready

### During the Inspection

1. Welcome the inspector and provide access to the animals
2. Answer any questions about your farm operations
3. Provide access to the app or printed records
4. Sign the inspection report at the end

## What Happens Next

- The inspection report is filed in the system
- If the farm passes, no further action is needed
- If corrective actions are required, you will receive a notice
- The inspection result influences next year's risk analysis
