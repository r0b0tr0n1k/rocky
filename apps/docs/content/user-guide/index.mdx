# User Guide Overview

Welcome to the Rocky livestock tracking system user guide. This guide is written for **farmers** and **veterinarians** in North Macedonia who use Rocky to manage cattle, sheep, and goat identification, movements, health records, and regulatory compliance.

## What You Can Do with Rocky

- **Register animals** — Record births and apply ear tags
- **Order ear tags** — Request and track tag deliveries
- **Record movements** — Log deaths, pasture changes, slaughter, sales, and cross-border movements
- **Manage health** — Record vaccinations, treatments, lab tests, and notifiable disease reports
- **Request passports** — Apply for and manage cattle passports
- **Track inspections** — View risk analysis and inspection results
- **Correct errors** — Fix data entry mistakes through the correction workflow
- **Stay compliant** — Meet all regulatory deadlines under EU and Macedonian law

## Who This Guide Is For

| Role | Primary App | Key Tasks |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| **Farmer** | Mobile app (iOS / Android) | Birth registration, ear tag orders, movement recording, passport requests |
| **Veterinarian** | Mobile app + Web Admin | Health records, disease reporting, inspections, corrections |
| **Farm Keeper** | Mobile app | Day-to-day animal management and movement recording |

## How to Use This Guide

Each section covers a specific workflow. Start with [Getting Started](/user-guide/getting-started) if you are new to Rocky, then explore the sections that match your role.

> **Tip:** All deadlines in this guide follow EU Implementing Regulation 2021/520 and Macedonian national law. When in doubt, record the event as soon as it happens — the system enforces the legal time windows.
