## Introduction

This document specifies the requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving an information security management system (ISMS) within the context of the organization. An ISMS is a systematic approach for managing sensitive information so that it remains secure, encompassing people, processes and information technology systems.

The controls in Annex A of this document provide a menu of information security controls that support the ISMS requirements. They are implemented, where relevant, through an organization's Statement of Applicability. Annex A of this edition is aligned by structure and content with ISO/IEC 27002:2022.

## 1 Scope

This document specifies the requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving an information security management system within the context of the organization. It also includes requirements for the assessment and treatment of information security risks tailored to the needs of the organization.

This document does not specify detailed technical implementation measures for any particular technology or business sector, nor does it prescribe a specific information security methodology.

## 3 Terms and definitions

For the purposes of this document, the following terms and definitions apply.

### 3.1 information security

preservation of confidentiality, integrity and availability of information.

### 3.2 information security management system (ISMS)

set of interrelated or interacting elements of an organization that establishes information security policies and objectives and enables the achievement of those objectives.

### 3.3 confidentiality, integrity, availability

the three core properties of information security: that information is not made available or disclosed to unauthorized individuals, entities or processes (confidentiality); that it is protected from unauthorized modification or destruction (integrity); and that it is accessible and usable upon demand by an authorized entity (availability).

### 3.4 control

a measure that modifies risk.

### 3.5 annex

a self-contained section of this document, designated normative or informative, that supplements the requirements of the main clauses.
