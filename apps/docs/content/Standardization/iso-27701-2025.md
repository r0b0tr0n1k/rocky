## Introduction

This document specifies requirements and provides guidance for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving a privacy information management system (PIMS). A PIMS is a set of interrelated elements — including policies, objectives, processes and controls — that an organization uses to manage its responsibilities for protecting personally identifiable information (PII).

ISO/IEC 27701:2025 is an extension to, and builds directly upon, ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 27002. It is not a standalone management-system standard; it adds privacy-specific requirements and controls (Annex A) and implementation guidance (Annex B) that supplement the information security controls of ISO/IEC 27001. Organizations seeking conformity with this document implement it together with ISO/IEC 27001.

## 1 Scope

This document specifies requirements and provides guidance for a PIMS based on the requirements of ISO/IEC 27001 and tailored to the processing of PII. It extends the requirements of ISO/IEC 27001 and the controls of ISO/IEC 27002 by adding requirements and controls specific to the role of a PII controller and/or a PII processor.

This document does not specify detailed technical implementation measures for any particular jurisdiction, nor does it override applicable legal, regulatory or contractual obligations relating to the processing of PII.

## 3 Terms and definitions

For the purposes of this document, the following terms and definitions apply.

### 3.1 personally identifiable information (PII)

Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person (PII principal).

### 3.2 PII principal

the natural person to whom the PII relates.

### 3.3 PII controller

the natural or legal person, public authority, agency or other body which, alone or jointly with others, determines the purposes and means of processing PII.

### 3.4 PII processor

the natural or legal person, public authority, agency or other body which processes PII on behalf of and in accordance with the instructions of a PII controller.

### 3.5 privacy information management system (PIMS)

that part of an organization's information security management system (ISMS) that addresses privacy by virtue of the application of privacy-specific controls.
