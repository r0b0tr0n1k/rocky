# ISO/IEC 27001:2022 — Annex A.5 (normative) — Organizational controls

## A.5.1

**Policies for information security**

Information security policy and topic-specific policies shall be defined, approved by management, published, communicated to and acknowledged by relevant personnel and relevant interested parties, and reviewed at planned intervals and if significant changes occur.
  
### Keywords

- a.5.1
- information security policy
- security policy
- review communications
- communicate communications
- approve communications
  
### Key Points

- Information security policy and topic-specific policies shall be defined, approved by management, published, communicated to and acknowledged by relevant personnel and relevant interested parties, and reviewed at planned intervals and if significant changes occur
  
### Compliance Checklist

- [ ] Policy must be approved by management
- [ ] Policy must be communicated to all relevant personnel
- [ ] Policy must be reviewed at planned intervals
- [ ] Security measures must be appropriate to risks
- [ ] Security measures must be documented
- [ ] Security incidents must be reported
