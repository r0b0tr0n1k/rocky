# Macedonian LPDP + GDPR + ISO 27701:2025 Implementation Guide

## Executive Summary

This implementation guide provides step-by-step instructions for organizations to achieve compliance with Macedonian Law on Personal Data Protection (LPDP), EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and ISO/IEC 27701:2025 Privacy Information Management. The guide includes practical templates, procedures, and checklists that organizations can immediately use.

## Policy Development

### Core Privacy Policy Framework

#### Step 4: Develop Privacy Policy

**Template: Privacy Policy**

```
Privacy Policy
Organization: [Organization Name]
Effective Date: [Date]
Version: 1.0

SCOPE:
This policy applies to all personal data processing activities conducted by [Organization Name] in North Macedonia and applies to all employees, contractors, and processors.

PURPOSE:
To ensure compliance with Macedonian LPDP, EU GDPR, and ISO 27701:2025 requirements while protecting the privacy rights of data subjects.

DATA PROTECTION PRINCIPLES:
1. Lawfulness, fairness and transparency
2. Purpose limitation
3. Data minimization
4. Accuracy
5. Storage limitation
6. Integrity and confidentiality
7. Accountability

DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS:
Individuals have the right to:
- Be informed about data processing
- Access their personal data
- Rectify inaccurate data
- Erase data under certain conditions
- Restrict processing
- Data portability
- Object to processing
- Not be subject to automated decision-making

LAWFUL BASIS FOR PROCESSING:
Processing is based on:
- Contract performance
- Legal obligation
- Vital interests
- Public tasks
- Legitimate interests
- Consent (where applicable)

DATA RETENTION:
Personal data is retained only as long as necessary for the processing purpose, subject to legal requirements.

DATA SHARING:
Personal data is shared only with authorized processors under appropriate agreements.

DATA SECURITY:
Appropriate technical and organizational measures protect personal data.

DATA BREACHES:
Personal data breaches are reported within 72 hours to authorities and affected individuals when required.

COMPLIANCE:
This policy is reviewed annually and updated as needed.

APPROVAL:
This policy has been approved by [Senior Management] on [Date].

[Signature]
[Name and Title]
[Date]
```

```
Data Processing Agreement
Controller: [Controller Name]
Processor: [Processor Name]
Effective Date: [Date]
Agreement ID: [ID]

PROCESSOR OBLIGATIONS:
1. Process personal data only on documented instructions of controller
2. Ensure appropriate technical and organizational measures
3. Provide sufficient guarantees for GDPR compliance
4. Process only authorized persons with confidentiality obligations
5. Assist controller with data subject rights fulfillment
6. Assist controller with data protection impact assessments
7. Notify controller without undue delay of personal data breaches
8. Delete or return personal data at end of processing
9. Submit to audits and inspections by controller or its auditor

- Technical measures as per ISO 27001 controls
- Organizational measures for data protection
- Regular security testing and assessment
- Incident response and breach notification procedures

SUB-PROCESSORS:
- List of authorized sub-processors provided
- Controller approval required for new sub-processors
- Same obligations applied to sub-processors
- Regular monitoring of sub-processor compliance

DATA TRANSFERS:
- Adequate safeguards for international transfers
- Compliance with Chapter V GDPR requirements
- Documentation of transfer mechanisms
- Regular assessment of adequacy

TERMINATION:
- Return or deletion of personal data at contract end
- Deletion certificate provided to controller
- Final compliance audit conducted
- Return of all confidential information

LIABILITY:
- Processor liable for non-compliance with agreement
- Compensation for damages caused by processor
- Insurance requirements if applicable

CONTROLLER RIGHTS:
- Right to audit processor compliance
- Right to inspect processing activities
- Right to require remediation of non-compliance
- Right to terminate for cause
```
