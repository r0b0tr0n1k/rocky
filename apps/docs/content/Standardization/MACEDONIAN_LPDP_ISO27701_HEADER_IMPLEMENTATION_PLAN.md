# Macedonian LPDP + ISO 27701:2025 Document Header Implementation Plan

## Executive Summary

This plan addresses the creation of document headers that prioritize **Macedonian LPDP (GDPR-compliant law) as the primary legal foundation**, with **ISO 27701:2025 as aspirational/secondary** target. The approach ensures Macedonian/EU companies have legally compliant headers while providing a clear pathway for ISO progression.

## Core Objectives

1. **Primary Focus**: Macedonian LPDP compliance as legal foundation
2. **Secondary Target**: ISO 27701:2025 mapping for aspirational compliance
3. **Clear Distinction**: Legal requirements vs ISO requirements in headers
4. **Strong Validation**: Headers that prevent gaps and ensure competent implementation
5. **Progressive Path**: Clear roadmap from legal compliance to ISO certification

## Implementation Architecture

### Phase 1: Macedonian LPDP Foundation Headers

* Legal compliance as primary requirement

* GDPR-based validation and control mapping
* Clear legal obligation identification
* Audit trail and evidence tracking

### Phase 2: Progressive ISO 27701:2025 Integration  

* Aspirational control mapping

* ISO progression indicators
* Maturity level tracking
* Gap analysis for ISO certification

### Phase 3: Quality Assurance and Validation

* Competency validation

* Gap detection and prevention
* Compliance verification
* Continuous improvement tracking

```yaml
# MACEDONIAN LPDP + ISO 27701:2025 COMPLIANCE HEADER
# Primary: Legal Compliance | Secondary: ISO 27701:2025 Mapping

# LEGAL COMPLIANCE FOUNDATION

  primary_standard: "Macedonian LPDP (Law 03/2008 - GDPR-compliant)"
  legal_references:
    - "Macedonian LPDP: Law No. 03/2008"
    - "GDPR: Regulation (EU) 2016/679"
    - "Local Implementation: Macedonian Data Protection Authority"
  legal_basis: "GDPR Article 6 lawful basis for processing"
  data_protection_officer: "Required for large-scale processing (GDPR Art. 37)"
  supervisory_authority: "Macedonian Data Protection Authority"
  
# LEGAL REQUIREMENTS MAPPING
legal_requirements:
  - article: "MK LPDP Art. 15"
    description: "Lawful basis for processing"
    legal_obligation: "MANDATORY - Legal basis must be established"
    evidence_required: ["Legal basis documentation", "Consent records", "Legitimate interest assessment"]
    compliance_level: "REQUIRED"
    
  - article: "MK LPDP Art. 17" 
    description: "Data subject rights"
    legal_obligation: "MANDATORY - Rights must be implemented"
    evidence_required: ["Rights request procedures", "Response procedures", "Rights documentation"]
    compliance_level: "REQUIRED"
    
  - article: "MK LPDP Art. 22"
    description: "Data Protection Impact Assessment"
    legal_obligation: "MANDATORY for high-risk processing"
    evidence_required: ["DPIA methodology", "Risk assessments", "Mitigation plans"]
    compliance_level: "CONDITIONAL"

# ISO 27701:2025 ASPIRATIONAL MAPPING
iso27701_mapping:
  implementation_level: "ASPIRATIONAL"
  primary_controls: ["A.1.2.2", "A.1.2.3", "A.1.3.1", "A.1.3.2"]
  maturity_level: "Level 1-5 (1=Initial, 5=Optimizing)"
  current_maturity: "Level 2 - Managed"
  target_maturity: "Level 4 - Quantitatively Managed"
  iso_gaps: ["A.2.2.2", "A.3.5", "A.3.15"]  # Areas needing ISO development
  progress_tracking: "Quarterly assessment against ISO 27701:2025 requirements"

# VALIDATION AND QUALITY ASSURANCE
validation:
  competency_checks:
    - "Legal basis clearly established"
    - "Data subject rights procedures implemented"
    - "DPIA conducted where required"
    - "Records of processing maintained"
  gap_analysis:
    legal_compliance_gaps: []  # Critical legal gaps
    iso_compliance_gaps: []    # ISO development areas
  audit_readiness:
    legal_audit_score: "85%"   # Percentage of legal requirements met
    iso_readiness_score: "60%" # Percentage of ISO controls implemented
  evidence_tracking:
    legal_evidence: ["Legal basis register", "DPIA records", "Rights request logs"]
    iso_evidence: ["ISO gap analysis", "Maturity assessments", "Control implementations"]
```
