# Logging And Monitoring Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the framework and requirements for maintaining secure operations and data protection in accordance with Article 36 of the Law on Personal Data Protection and Article 28 of Law No. 124/2024 on Personal Data Protection.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All [Company Name] employees, contractors, and third parties
- All information systems, networks, and physical facilities
- All data processing activities
- All locations and operational environments

## 3. Policy Statement

[Company Name] is committed to:

1. Maintaining compliance with applicable laws and regulations
2. Implementing appropriate security measures
3. Ensuring protection of personal data
4. Providing ongoing training and awareness
5. Conducting regular reviews and updates
6. Maintaining audit trails and documentation

## 4. Key Requirements

### 4.1 Risk Management

- Conduct regular risk assessments
- Implement appropriate security controls
- Monitor and review control effectiveness
- Document all risk management activities

### 4.2 Technical Security

- Implement access controls and authentication
- Use encryption for sensitive data
- Maintain secure backup and recovery procedures
- Regularly update and patch systems

### 4.3 Physical Security

- Control access to facilities
- Implement visitor management procedures
- Secure equipment and storage areas
- Maintain surveillance systems

### 4.4 Incident Management

- Establish incident response procedures
- Train personnel on incident handling
- Document and report incidents
- Conduct post-incident reviews

## 5. Responsibilities

### 5.1 Management

- Approve and support policy implementation
- Allocate necessary resources for compliance
- Ensure policy review and updates
- Promote security awareness

### 5.2 IT Operations Department

- Implement policy requirements
- Monitor compliance
- Report policy violations
- Conduct regular audits

### 5.3 Data Protection Officer

- Provide guidance on policy implementation
- Monitor regulatory changes
- Coordinate with relevant authorities
- Conduct compliance reviews

### 5.4 All Employees

- Comply with policy requirements
- Report security incidents
- Complete mandatory training
- Protect company and personal data

## 6. Compliance and Enforcement

All personnel must comply with this policy. Non-compliance may result in:

- Disciplinary action
- Revocation of access privileges
- Legal liability
- Regulatory penalties
- Termination of employment or contract

## 7. Review and Updates

This policy shall be reviewed:

- Annually or upon significant changes
- Following any data protection incidents
- When legal or regulatory requirements change
- At minimum every 12 months

## 8. Records and Documentation

Maintain the following records:

- Policy approval and communications (Permanent)
- Compliance monitoring reports (5 years)
- Training records (3 years)
- Incident reports (10 years)
- Audit findings (5 years)

Records must be stored securely and retained according to the retention policy.
