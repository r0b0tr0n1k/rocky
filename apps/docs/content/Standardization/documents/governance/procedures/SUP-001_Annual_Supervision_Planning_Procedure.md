
# SUP-001 Annual Supervision Planning Procedure

## Purpose

This procedure establishes the methodology for developing the Annual Program for Conducting Supervision over personal data controllers, in accordance with Article 104, paragraph (7) of the Law on Personal Data Protection and the Regulation on the Manner of Conducting Supervision.

## Scope

This procedure applies to the Agency for Personal Data Protection supervision planning activities.

## Legal Basis


* MK_LPDP Article 104, paragraph (7)

* Albanian LAW124 Article 82, paragraph 1, letter 'd'

* Macedonian Supervision Rulebook Articles 3-5


## Responsibilities


### Director of the Agency

* Approves the Annual Program for Conducting Supervision

* Ensures compliance with legal requirements


### Supervision Sector

* Develops the draft Annual Program

* Conducts risk assessments

* Analyzes statistical data and complaints



## Procedure


### 1. Risk Assessment Methodology

The Annual Program must be developed based on a comprehensive risk assessment considering:



#### 1.1 Breach History Analysis
- Review reported personal data security breaches
- Analyze breach patterns and root causes
- Identify high-risk sectors prone to breaches

#### 1.2 Statistical Data Review
- Analyze results from previous supervision activities
- Identify compliance trends and recurring violations
- Review controller response times and remediation effectiveness



### 2. Program Development Process





#### 2.1 Draft Preparation (December 1-20)
The Supervision Sector shall:
- Collect and analyze all risk factors
- Propose proportional allocation of supervision resources
- Ensure balanced coverage across sectors and controller sizes
- Include both regular and extraordinary supervision activities




## Records

### Required Documentation

* Risk assessment reports and analysis

* Draft Annual Program with justification

* Director approval documentation

* Published Annual Program

* Amendment records with justifications


### Retention Period
* Maintain records for 7 years minimum
