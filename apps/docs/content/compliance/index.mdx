# Compliance Posture

This section holds Rocky's conformity posture for its Information Security Management System (ISMS)
and Privacy Information Management System (PIMS). The canonical Statement of Applicability and spine
for the whole posture is [`isms-policy.md` (ROCKY-ISMS-001)](isms-policy.md) — it carries the full
Annex A.5–A.8 + PIMS A.1–A.3 control table and is the reference every other posture document cites.
The machine-readable standard texts and crosswalks live in the
[`Standardization/`](../Standardization/index.md) tree.

The governing roadmap for this posture is
[ADR-0067 — ISMS Posture & ISO 27001 / ISO 27701:2025 Conformity Roadmap](../ADR/0067-isms-posture-iso27001-27701-roadmap.md);
the system-architecture diagram set that underpins the posture is governed by ADR-0104.

## Conformity claim

Rocky asserts conformity to the laws and regulations that govern its processing of personal data. The
applicable legal baseline is quoted verbatim in the artifact set below and is the basis for the claim:

- **GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679)** — the EU general data-protection law. Conformity is evidenced by
the Art 30 RoPA ([ROCKY-ROPA-001](rocky-ropa.md)), the Art 28 DPA ([ROCKY-PROC-001](rocky-dpa.md)),
the Art 33/34 breach flow ([ROCKY-BRCH-001](rocky-breach-notification-procedure.md)), the Art 17
erasure + retention flow ([ROCKY-ERP-001](rocky-erasure-retention-procedure.md)), the Art 6 lawful-basis
map ([ROCKY-LBR-001](rocky-lawful-basis-register.md)), the Art 12–22 DSR flow
([ROCKY-DSR-001](rocky-dsr-procedure.md)), and the Art 13/14 transparency notice
([Privacy Notice](rocky-privacy-notice.md)).
- **Macedonian LPDP (Law on Personal Data Protection, MK)** — the in-scope MK jurisdiction. The MK
articles are quoted verbatim in [MACEDONIAN_LPDP.md](MACEDONIAN_LPDP.md) and crosswalked to GDPR in
the [control matrix](MACEDONIAN_LPDP.md); the bilingual source law is carried as
[`mk_lpdp_bilingual_en_mk.json`](../Standardization/mk_lpdp_bilingual_en_mk.json).
- **Albanian Law 124** — explicitly **out of scope** for this posture (carried from a separate project;
not part of Rocky's conformity claim). It is retained only as a future `VALIDATED_CROSSWALK` leg and is
not cited as an obligation.

The claim is made against the verbatim law, not a paraphrase: the legal artifacts above quote the
applicable articles word-for-word and map each to the control that gives it effect in
[`isms-policy.md` (ROCKY-ISMS-001)](isms-policy.md). The ISO/IEC *published standards* (27001, 27701,
etc.) are referenced and crosswalked, not reproduced, per their copyright.
