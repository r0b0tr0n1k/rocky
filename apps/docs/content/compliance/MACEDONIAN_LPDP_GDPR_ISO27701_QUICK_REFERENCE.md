# Macedonian LPDP + GDPR + ISO 27701:2025 Quick Reference Guide

## Compliance Overview

### Legal Framework Alignment

* **Macedonian LPDP**: National data protection law (aligns with GDPR)
* **GDPR**: EU regulation with extraterritorial application  
* **ISO 27701:2025**: Privacy extension to ISO 27001 ISMS standard
* **Penalties**: Up to 4% of annual turnover for non-compliance

## Key Requirements Summary

### 1. Governance & Accountability

* **Appoint DPO** (Art 37-39 MK LPDP / Art 37-39 GDPR)
* **Document processing activities** (Art 30 MK LPDP / Art 30 GDPR)
* **Implement privacy by design** (Art 25 MK LPDP / Art 25 GDPR)
* **Conduct PIAs** when high risk (Art 35-36 MK LPDP / Art 35-36 GDPR)

### 2. Data Subject Rights

* **Right of access** (SAR) - respond within 1 month
* **Right to rectification** - correct inaccurate data
* **Right to erasure** - delete when no legal basis
* **Right to restrict processing** - limit processing activities
* **Right to data portability** - provide in structured format
* **Right to object** - to processing under legitimate interests

### 3. Technical & Organizational Measures

* **Appropriate security** for personal data (Art 32 MK LPDP / Art 32 GDPR)
* **Access controls** based on need-to-know principle
* **Data encryption** for protection at rest and in transit
* **Logging** of all personal data access and modifications
* **Breach notification** within 72 hours to authorities

### 4. Vendor & Processor Management

* **Written agreements** with processors (Art 28 MK LPDP / Art 28 GDPR)
* **Prior authorization** for sub-processors
* **Processor compliance monitoring**
* **Data breach notification** by processors to controllers
* **Return/deletion** of data at contract end

## ISO 27701:2025 Control Mappings

### A.1.x Series - PII Controller Requirements

* **A.1.2.1**: Establish PII controller obligations
* **A.1.2.2**: Identify and document processing purposes
* **A.1.2.3**: Determine and document legal basis
* **A.1.2.4**: Determine consent processes
* **A.1.2.6**: Implement Privacy Impact Assessments
* **A.1.3.1-A.1.3.7**: Implement data subject rights procedures
* **A.1.4.1**: Privacy by design and by default

### A.5.x Series - Organizational Controls

* **A.5.1**: Information security policies (enhanced for privacy)
* **A.5.2**: Information security roles and responsibilities
* **A.5.15**: Access control (enhanced for personal data)
* **A.5.26**: Information security incident management (breach response)
* **A.5.31**: Legal, regulatory and contractual requirements

### B.1.x, B.2.x Series - PII Processor Requirements

* **B.2.2.1**: Process only on controller instructions
* **B.2.2.2**: Customer agreement requirements
* **B.2.2.3**: Sub-processor controls
* **B.2.4.1**: Personal data breach notification to controller

## Data Subject Rights Implementation

### Right of Access (SAR) Process

1. **Receive request** via designated channels
2. **Verify identity** of requester (no excessive verification)
3. **Search systems** for relevant personal data
4. **Prepare response** with required information
5. **Respond within 1 month** (extendable to 2 months if complex)
6. **Document process** and maintain records

### Breach Response Timeline

* **Detect breach**: Immediate notification to DPO
* **Assess risk**: Within 24 hours of detection
* **Notify authority**: Within 72 hours if high risk
* **Notify individuals**: When high risk to rights/freedoms
* **Document**: All actions taken during response

## Common Implementation Pitfalls to Avoid

### ❌ Don't Do

* Implement privacy controls in isolation
* Ignore processor obligations if you're a processor
* Overlook the documentation requirements
* Fail to involve IT security team early
* Delay staff training implementation

### ✅ Do Instead

* Integrate privacy with existing security programs
* Implement appropriate controls for your role
* Document everything systematically
* Engage IT team from project start
* Train staff early and regularly

## Emergency Procedures

### Data Breach (Immediate - Within 1 Hour)

1. **Contain** the breach to prevent further exposure
2. **Assess** scope and impact of the breach
3. **Notify** DPO and incident response team
4. **Document** initial findings and actions
5. **Preserve** evidence for investigation

### SAR Received (Within 1 Business Day)

1. **Acknowledge** receipt to requester
2. **Verify** identity of requester
3. **Log** request in SAR tracking system
4. **Assign** to appropriate response team
5. **Begin** search for requested data
