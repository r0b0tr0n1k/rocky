## Who we are

Rocky is the livestock identification, movement, health and inspection information
system operated on behalf of the competent veterinary / food-safety authority
(the **controller**) under veterinary official control. The controller determines
the purposes and means of processing.

## What we process and why

- **Purposes:** animal identification & traceability, disease surveillance,
  movement / death / slaughter control, on-site inspection.
- **Lawful basis:** Article 6(1)(e) — task in the public interest / official
  authority. We do **not** rely on your consent.
- **Personal data:** your name, national ID, contact details and farm location
  (keeper / veterinarian / officer). Animal **health** records (diagnosis,
  treatment, vaccination) are special-category data processed under
  Article 9(2)(g) / 9(2)(i) (public health / substantial public interest).

## Who we share it with

Veterinary stations (VS), inspection bodies (VI) and the central archive (CPC);
and processors — Better Auth (authentication), EAS / Expo (delivery), and the
database host. Transfers are domestic; any sub-processor outside North Macedonia
is covered by Article 46 / 49 safeguards (see ROCKY-PROC-001).

## How long we keep it

- Inspection & archive documents: **3 years**.
- Treatment / vaccination records: **≥ 5 years** (veterinary-medicines floor).
- Movement records: **3–7 years** (traceability floor).
- **You cannot be erased while you own a living regulated animal.** Once no
  living animal remains and the retention floor has expired, your identity data
  is erased and the traceability is de-identified. See ROCKY-ERP-001.

## Your rights

You may request **access**, **rectification**, **erasure** (subject to the legal
retention above), **restriction**, **objection**, and **data portability**. You
may also lodge a complaint with the Macedonian Data Protection Agency
(Дирекција за заштита на личните податоци). The Data Protection Officer is
`<to be designated>`; the request channel is `<to be published>`.
